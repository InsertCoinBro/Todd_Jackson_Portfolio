Dear Hiring Team,

I thrive in environments where challenges demand calm, structure, and creative problem-solving. For more than seven years, I have worked in customer-facing and leadership roles, including crisis intervention, where clarity, empathy, and sound judgment matter in every interaction. That experience has taught me to think quickly, adapt to changing conditions, and guide people toward stability when the stakes are high.

My approach to leadership centers on developing people and improving systems. I have streamlined counselor workflows, resolved technical issues during live operations, built knowledge-base resources, and helped new staff become confident responders. I look for the point where a process can become clearer, more reliable, or easier for the person using it.

As I transition into software engineering, I bring that same focus on practical solutions and human needs. I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering and building my foundation in semantic HTML, CSS, JavaScript debugging, and application development. My goal is to combine technical growth with the judgment, communication, and persistence I have developed in high-impact service environments.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute this blend of crisis leadership, technical troubleshooting, and systems thinking to a team that values dependable work and continuous learning. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
Todd Jackson
