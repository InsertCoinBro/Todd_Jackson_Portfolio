Where I’m headed

Building useful technology around real human needs.

My long-term goal is to create practical software that helps people navigate complex moments with more clarity—especially in healthcare, financial wellness, and crisis-support environments.

My direction

From crisis leadership to software engineering

Working directly with people through 988 crisis services showed me the value of tools that are reliable, intuitive, and built with care. I am now developing the technical skills to create those kinds of tools myself.

I am especially interested in applications that reduce friction for users and support the professionals serving them. A future goal is to build software that helps crisis counselors manage the practical demands of a shift without losing focus on the person in front of them.
